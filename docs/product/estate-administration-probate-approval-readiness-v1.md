# Estate Administration probate candidate pack — approval readiness v1

## Status and boundary

This is a review-readiness packet for draft England and Wales probate and
Letters of Administration knowledge candidates. It is not a complete probate
guide, legal advice, approval evidence, a production profile, or activation
authority.

Every checked-in candidate remains:

- `draft`;
- `evidenceConfidence: "blocked"`;
- limited to `england_and_wales`;
- assigned to an explicitly non-production profile;
- unreviewed and unapproved;
- absent from the activation manifest;
- excluded from runtime projection, routing, and UI.

The pack helps a future reviewer inspect small propositions. It does not decide
whether a grant is required, who may apply, which grant or form applies, whether
a will is valid, whether tax is due, how an asset passes, or what should happen
in an individual estate.

## Scope

Included:

- bounded official terminology;
- the ordinary distinction between grant types;
- ordinary will and no-will applicant descriptions, with priority and authority
  left unresolved;
- institution-specific grant requirements and the absence of a universal
  estate-value test in the researched official guidance;
- cautious joint-asset preparation wording;
- the high-level relationship between valuation, Inheritance Tax reporting, and
  a later grant application;
- route-neutral preparation and application-route descriptions;
- the no-acting-executor boundary;
- the distinction between receiving a grant and completing administration.

Excluded:

- personalised grant-need, grant-type, authority, priority, will-validity,
  survivorship, ownership, tax, solvency, debt, inheritance, distribution,
  deadline, or outcome decisions;
- the full Rule 20 or Rule 22 priority hierarchies as product logic;
- probate fees, processing-time promises, contact details, and fixed form
  recommendations;
- caveat or dispute tactics;
- detailed renunciation, power-reserved, attorney, capacity, minority, trust,
  foreign, domicile, prior-grant, insolvent-estate, property-sale, tax, debt, or
  distribution workflows;
- Scotland, Northern Ireland, Crown Dependencies, and overseas estates;
- collection of private estate documents or identifiers.

## Required uncertainty and escalation

The pack must remain uncertain about:

- jurisdiction where England and Wales has not been established;
- whether a will exists, is valid, current, complete, or controlling;
- whether a person is an executor, administrator, personal representative, or
  entitled applicant;
- whether any grant or particular grant type is required;
- asset ownership, survivorship, trusts, nominations, pensions, and foreign
  assets;
- institution thresholds and evidence requirements;
- estate valuation, Inheritance Tax reporting, payment, liability, and probate
  codes;
- estate debts, solvency, beneficiary entitlement, and distribution;
- route/form eligibility and application completeness.

The ordinary explanations are insufficient where there is a dispute, missing or
altered original will, unclear ownership, no acting executor, capacity or
minority issue, competing applicant, trust, foreign element, unusual ownership,
possible insolvency, prior grant, urgent transaction, or active legal
correspondence. Those circumstances require current official information and
may require a solicitor or authorised probate practitioner.

## Claims deliberately blocked

AdminAvenger must not say:

- probate is definitely required or unnecessary;
- a value or institution threshold decides the whole estate;
- a named person is entitled to apply or has legal authority;
- a will is valid, invalid, latest, revoked, or controlling;
- an asset passes by survivorship;
- an institution must release money or accept a document;
- Inheritance Tax is due, not due, paid, or settled;
- the estate is solvent or ready for distribution;
- an executor can be removed or replaced through a simple step;
- a particular form, renunciation, power-reserved, attorney, or caveat choice is
  correct;
- an estimate is a deadline or guaranteed completion time;
- receiving a grant means the estate is finished.

## Privacy boundary

These candidates explain categories only. A person does not need to provide
AdminAvenger with names, addresses, account numbers, National Insurance
numbers, tax references, bank details, wills, death certificates, identity
documents, or full asset schedules to receive a general explanation.

No flow, storage, upload, form completion, document generation, or private
estate data is added by this pack.

## Freshness and re-review

All candidates record a source verification date of 30 July 2026 and
`validUntil: null`. The non-production profile requires a valid-until date, so
every candidate fails closed even if every other gate were somehow satisfied.

A real reviewer must propose and approve claim-appropriate expiry treatment.
Re-review is required after:

- a GOV.UK Applying for probate guide change;
- an HMCTS route, form, evidence, or service change;
- an affecting provision for the Administration of Estates Act 1925;
- an amendment to the Non-Contentious Probate Rules;
- a jurisdiction or terminology change;
- any change to a claim, source snapshot, wording, qualifier, exception,
  prohibited conclusion, profile, or consumption scope.

Any approval-relevant change alters the canonical digest. Once review begins,
the changed content needs a new immutable revision and renewed evidence.

## Human review requirements

The profile is `estate_administration_probate_non_production_v1`. It is
explicitly non-production and requires independent evidence for:

1. evidence accuracy;
2. qualified probate/domain review;
3. product safety and emotional safety;
4. accessibility;
5. privacy and data minimisation;
6. product scope and permitted use;
7. engine-use constraints;
8. freshness and expiry;
9. separate activation authority.

There are no completed review requests, eligibility records, assignments,
findings, decisions, signatures, or approval records. A future reviewer must use
the blank workflow in
`estate-administration-human-review-workflow-v1.md`; blank or checked-in text is
not evidence.

## Approval and activation blockers

Before any candidate could be approved, humans would need to:

1. re-open every relevant primary source;
2. confirm exact source scope, wording, date, and limitations;
3. complete qualified legal/probate, safety, accessibility, privacy, scope,
   engine-use, and freshness review;
4. approve a versioned production profile and expiry policy;
5. freeze the exact revisions and verify the canonical digests;
6. create valid review requests, eligible reviewer records, accepted
   assignments, and external evidence for every profile role;
7. resolve or remove every condition and blocked claim;
8. separately approve editorial disposition and product scope.

Approval would still not activate a candidate. A later, separately authorised
change would need an exact activation-manifest pin and approved product routing.
Neither exists.
