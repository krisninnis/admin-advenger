# Public-Message Adversarial Evaluation Sources v1

This inventory informs synthetic adversarial *patterns*. It is not a production
knowledge corpus, legal or benefits advice, or evidence that AdminAvenger may
decide entitlement, liability, rights or outcomes. Fixtures use invented names,
references, dates, amounts and wording.

Freshness classes are harness-maintenance prompts: **time-sensitive** means
recheck before a related corpus change, **operational** means recheck during a
scheduled harness review, and **stable-reference** means the page supplies a
general distinction rather than a current deadline or rate. “Time-sensitive”
does not authorise automated web access or product activation.
